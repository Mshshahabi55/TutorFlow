## What was NOT captured, and why

- **The authenticated 8-step wizard itself** (About → Photo → Certification → Education → Description → Video → Availability → Pricing) was **not reachable**. `/en/tutor-signup` redirects anonymous visitors to `/en/teach` (SS59). No Preply account exists for this project and none was created — creating one was explicitly out of scope per `00-scope-and-methodology.md` and the current task's instructions (no fake information submitted, no registration finalized, no login credentials requested from or provided by the user).
- **Google/Facebook OAuth signup buttons exist on the form** (visible in SS54/SS57) but were **not clicked** — doing so would attempt to create or sign into a real third-party account, which is out of scope for an unauthenticated evidence-capture task.
- **Malformed-input validation** (bad email format, weak password, etc.) was not tested — only the blank/empty-field case was triggered, since that is the only validation state reachable without entering any real or fabricated personal data.
- **"Remember me" checkbox, password-reveal toggle, and language/currency selector** were visible but not exercised — clicking them does not touch the wizard content itself and was judged out of scope for this capture.
